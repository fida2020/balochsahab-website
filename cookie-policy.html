Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used on this website.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may use local storage, pixels, or identifiers to remember preferences, support functionality, understand usage, or maintain security.

How this site may use them

We may use strictly necessary technologies to operate the site, preference storage to remember choices such as display settings, and analytics technologies to understand aggregate website use.

Necessary technologies

Some browser storage may be required for requested features, security, navigation, or saving a preference. Blocking these technologies can affect how the site works.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled, it may collect information such as visited pages, approximate location, device type, referral source, and interaction events. The exact data depends on the analytics provider and configuration.

Third-party content

Links to third-party websites do not place their cookies through our pages by themselves, but those sites may use cookies after you visit them. Their policies govern their use.

Your controls

You can delete or block cookies and site data using browser settings. Some browsers also offer tracking protection or private browsing controls. These settings may need to be managed separately on each device.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website technology or legal obligations change.
