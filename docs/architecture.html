Automation Architecture

A modular model for connecting inputs, intelligence, approvals, actions, and observability.

01

Inputs

Forms, schedules, content briefs, databases, webhooks, and platform events start the workflow.

02

Orchestration

n8n or a custom service routes data, applies rules, and coordinates each step.

03

Intelligence

AI models support research, classification, drafting, transformation, and decision assistance.

04

Human Review

Approval gates keep judgment visible before sensitive or public actions.

05

Actions

Connected services store assets, publish content, update records, and notify people.

06

Observability

Statuses, logs, alerts, and metrics show what happened and what needs attention.

Design principles

Workflows are separated into clear modules so one integration can change without forcing a complete rebuild. Credentials remain in appropriate secret stores, data collection is limited to what the process needs, and failures are routed to visible recovery paths.

Automation is most dependable when every action is idempotent where practical, retries are controlled, and a person can inspect the state before restarting a failed stage.

Deployment approach

A pilot validates the data flow and output quality. Production rollout adds monitoring, ownership, documentation, and a change process. Capacity and infrastructure depend on workload, integrations, privacy requirements, and the service level expected.
