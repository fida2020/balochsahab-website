Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Baloch Sahab Automation (“the Tool,” “I,” or “me”) handles information in connection with this personal social media automation tool available at balochsahab.com.

Baloch Sahab Automation is a personal tool used solely to create and publish original video content to my own authorized social media accounts. It is not a public service and does not manage or publish content on behalf of any other user or account.

2. Information Collected

Because this Tool is for personal use only, it does not operate as a multi-user platform. Information that may be processed includes:

  • Account credentials and authorization tokens for social platforms I personally connect (for example, TikTok), solely to publish to my own accounts
  • Content I create or upload for publishing (such as videos, captions, and related metadata)
  • Technical logs needed to operate and debug the Tool (such as timestamps and error messages)

I do not knowingly collect personal information from other individuals through this website or Tool.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Authenticate with platforms I authorize
  • Create, schedule, and publish original content to my own accounts
  • Maintain security and reliability of the Tool

4. Sharing of Information

I do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the Tool (for example, with a social platform’s API when publishing), or when required by law.

5. Third-Party Services

When connected to platforms such as TikTok, their respective privacy policies and terms also apply. Access can be revoked through those platforms’ account settings at any time.

6. Data Retention & Security

Authorization data and related materials are retained only as long as needed to operate the Tool. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Children’s Privacy

This Tool is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
