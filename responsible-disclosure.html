Responsible Disclosure

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

Baloch Sahab Automation welcomes good-faith security research that helps us protect customers and the integrity of balochsahab.com and related services. This policy explains how to report vulnerabilities responsibly.

2. Scope

This program covers security issues in Baloch Sahab Automation-owned web properties and the public SaaS application. Out of scope:

  • Third-party platforms (TikTok, Meta, Google, etc.) — report to those vendors directly
  • Social engineering or physical attacks against staff or facilities
  • Denial-of-service attacks or automated scanning that degrades service for others
  • Issues requiring physical access to a user's device

3. How to report

Send vulnerability details to support@balochsahab.com with the subject line Security Disclosure. Include:

  • Description of the vulnerability and potential impact
  • Steps to reproduce (proof of concept if available)
  • Affected URLs or components
  • Your contact information for follow-up

4. Our commitment

We will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within five business days
  • Investigate reported issues in good faith
  • Keep you informed of significant progress where appropriate
  • Not pursue legal action against researchers who comply with this policy

5. Safe harbor guidelines

To qualify for safe harbor, please:

  • Do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to other customers
  • Do not exploit vulnerabilities beyond what is necessary to demonstrate impact
  • Do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had reasonable time to remediate (typically 90 days, or sooner by mutual agreement)
  • Act in good faith to avoid privacy violations and service disruption

6. Recognition

We appreciate researchers who help improve our security posture. With your permission, we may acknowledge your contribution in release notes or a security acknowledgments page.

7. Contact

Report vulnerabilities: support@balochsahab.com · General security questions: same address · WhatsApp +92 321 8818909
