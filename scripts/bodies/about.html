About

Premium automation with a clear purpose

Baloch Sahab Automation builds public SaaS tools that help creators and businesses publish with consistency and polish.

Our story

Social media operations should feel luxurious — calm, reliable, and visually refined — not chaotic. We founded Baloch Sahab Automation to give creators, agencies, and enterprises a single platform for AI-assisted planning, creation, scheduling, and publishing across TikTok and major networks.

What we build

A public AI-powered social media automation product covering content planning, creation support, multi-platform publishing, analytics, and secure brand workspaces. Every plan from Starter to Enterprise is designed for operators who take brand presence seriously.

Who we serve

Solo creators scaling personal brands, agencies managing client portfolios, startups building audience early, and enterprises coordinating global social presence. If you need dependable automation that respects platform rules, we built this for you.

Mission & vision

Our mission is to replace repetitive publishing chores with disciplined, elegant workflows. Our vision is to become the most trusted public SaaS platform for compliant, premium social automation worldwide.
