Our story

Baloch Sahab is an online earning and rewards platform. We give people a simple way to earn money in their spare time by completing real, verified activities — watching ads, visiting shortlinks, completing offerwall tasks, and checking in daily — with every reward confirmed before it reaches your balance.

What we build

Our first product is EarnBox, a real Android app for watching rewarded video ads, completing offers and games from our partner networks, claiming a daily bonus, and earning a referral commission. This website extends the same real earning platform to the web.

How it works

Every reward on Baloch Sahab is verified server-to-server with our ad and offer network partners before it's credited — we never trust a claim without a real, confirmed completion. What you see in your wallet reflects real, verified activity, never an estimate.
