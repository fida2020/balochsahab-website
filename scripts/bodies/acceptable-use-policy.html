Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs use of Baloch Sahab Automation services. By using our platform, you agree to comply with this AUP, our Terms of Service, and all applicable laws.

2. Lawful use

You must use the service only for lawful purposes. You may not use Baloch Sahab Automation to facilitate fraud, harassment, hate speech, illegal activity, or infringement of intellectual property or privacy rights.

3. No spam or abusive messaging

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Unsolicited bulk messaging, spam campaigns, or deceptive outreach

  • Phishing, malware distribution, or social engineering

When connecting social accounts, you must comply with each platform's terms, developer policies, and community guidelines. This includes but is not limited to:

5. Prohibited content

You may not upload, schedule, or publish content that is illegal, sexually exploitative, violent, defamatory, or that promotes dangerous or harmful activities. Content involving minors must comply with all applicable child safety laws.

6. Account security

You must protect login credentials and API tokens. Sharing accounts in ways that violate platform rules, reselling unauthorized access, or attempting to bypass security controls is prohibited.

7. Technical abuse

You may not probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of our systems without authorization. Reverse engineering, scraping beyond permitted API use, or interfering with service availability for other customers is forbidden.

8. Enforcement

Violations may result in warning, content removal, feature restriction, account suspension, or termination without refund, depending on severity. We may report illegal activity to authorities where required.

9. Reporting violations

Report suspected abuse to support@balochsahab.com with relevant details. We review reports in good faith.
