Who we are

Baloch Sahab Automation develops and operates a public software-as-a-service platform that helps teams plan, create, schedule, and publish content across TikTok and major social networks — with the polish and reliability expected of a luxury-grade product.

What we do

Our platform combines AI-assisted content drafting, timezone-aware scheduling, multi-platform publishing, brand workspaces, analytics, and secure access controls. We serve solo creators, agencies, startups, and enterprises who need consistent, compliant automation.
