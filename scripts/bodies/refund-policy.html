Refund Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how Baloch Sahab Automation handles refunds and billing adjustments for paid subscriptions to our public SaaS platform at balochsahab.com.

2. Subscription billing

Paid plans (Starter, Professional, Business) are billed on a recurring monthly basis unless otherwise agreed in writing. Enterprise agreements follow separately negotiated terms.

3. General refund approach

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun. We do not provide prorated refunds for partial months, unused features, or downgrades mid-cycle, except where required by applicable law.

4. Exceptions we may consider

We may review refund or credit requests on a case-by-case basis in situations such as:

  • Duplicate charges or clear billing errors verified by our billing team
  • Extended service unavailability directly attributable to our platform (see our Service Level Agreement for context)
  • Charges made after a verified cancellation request processed incorrectly
  • Legal requirements in your jurisdiction mandating a refund

5. Free trials and promotional offers

If a free trial or promotional discount is offered, specific terms will be stated at the time of signup. Promotional pricing does not create an entitlement to future refunds at the standard rate.

6. How to request a review

Contact billing@balochsahab.com with your account email, invoice reference, and a brief explanation. Include any relevant dates or transaction IDs. We aim to respond within five business days.

7. Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your payment provider, please contact billing so we can investigate. Unresolved chargebacks may result in account suspension pending review.

8. Cancellations

You may cancel a monthly subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the end of the current paid period unless otherwise stated in your plan terms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
