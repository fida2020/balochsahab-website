Refund Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Overview

Baloch Sahab is a free earning and rewards platform — you do not pay us to use it, so there are no subscription charges to refund. This policy explains how reward reversals and withdrawal issues are handled instead.

2. Reward reversals

Rewards are credited only after our ad and offer network partners verify a completion. If a partner later reports that a completion was invalid, fraudulent, or a duplicate, the corresponding reward may be reversed from your balance.

3. Withdrawal issues

If a withdrawal is rejected (for example, due to incorrect account details or a failed identity check), the amount is returned to your available balance so you can correct the details and request again.

4. How to request a review

If you believe a reward was reversed or a withdrawal was rejected in error, contact support@balochsahab.com with your account email and the relevant transaction details. We aim to respond within five business days.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
