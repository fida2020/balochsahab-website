Security Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Our commitment

Baloch Sahab is an online earning and rewards platform. Protecting your account, your balance, and the integrity of every reward is central to how we design and operate our services.

2. Data protection

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal and account data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit
  • Access controls limiting employee and system access to production data
  • Password hashing — we never store passwords in plain text
  • Regular review of infrastructure and dependency security

3. Authentication and access

Accounts require authenticated access with a hashed password or Google Sign-In. We encourage strong, unique passwords. Session and token handling follows industry-standard practices.

4. Reward verification

Every reward is verified server-to-server with our ad and offer network partners before it's credited to your balance — nothing is credited from a client-side claim alone.

5. Fraud and abuse prevention

We monitor for patterns consistent with multiple-accounting, automated completions, and other abuse. Accounts engaged in prohibited activity may have rewards reversed and be restricted or suspended.

6. Withdrawals

Withdrawals require identity verification. We review withdrawal requests for signs of fraud before payment.

7. Incident response

If we become aware of a security incident affecting user data, we will investigate promptly and notify affected users where required by law.

8. Responsible disclosure

Security researchers are welcome to report vulnerabilities through our Responsible Disclosure process. We request good-faith reporting and reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure.

9. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for securing your login credentials and for the accuracy of the withdrawal details you provide.
