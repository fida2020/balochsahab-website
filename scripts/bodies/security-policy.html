Security Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Our commitment

Baloch Sahab Automation is built as a public SaaS platform for social media automation. Protecting customer data, connected accounts, and platform integrity is central to how we design and operate our services.

2. Data protection

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal and account data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit
  • Access controls limiting employee and system access to production data
  • Secure credential handling for connected platform tokens
  • Regular review of infrastructure and dependency security

3. Authentication and access

Customer accounts require authenticated access. We encourage strong passwords and recommend that teams use role-based permissions within brand workspaces. Session handling follows industry-standard practices.

4. Infrastructure

Our platform runs on reputable cloud infrastructure with monitoring for availability and anomalous activity. Backups and recovery procedures are maintained for critical service data.

5. Third-party integrations

When you connect TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, or other networks, we access only the permissions required to deliver scheduling and publishing features. We do not sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

6. Acceptable use and abuse prevention

We monitor for patterns consistent with abuse, spam, or policy violations as described in our Acceptable Use Policy. Accounts engaged in prohibited activity may be restricted or suspended.

7. Incident response

If we become aware of a security incident affecting customer data, we will investigate promptly and notify affected customers where required by law or contract. Enterprise customers may receive enhanced incident communication per agreement.

8. Responsible disclosure

Security researchers are welcome to report vulnerabilities through our Responsible Disclosure process. We request good-faith reporting and reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure.

9. Customer responsibilities

You are responsible for securing your login credentials, reviewing connected platform permissions, and ensuring your team complies with platform rules and our policies.
