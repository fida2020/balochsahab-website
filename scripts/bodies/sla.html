Service Level Agreement

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") describes service availability targets for Baloch Sahab Automation. Unless explicitly included in a signed Enterprise agreement, this SLA is informational and does not create a contractual guarantee.

2. Service availability target

We target 99.9% uptime for core platform services measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced in advance. This is an operational goal — not a guaranteed service level for all plans.

3. What counts as downtime

Downtime refers to periods when authenticated customers cannot access the primary web application or scheduled publishing pipeline due to a failure within our infrastructure. Downtime does not include:

  • Scheduled maintenance with reasonable prior notice
  • Issues caused by third-party platforms (e.g., TikTok API outages)
  • Customer network, browser, or device problems
  • Force majeure events beyond reasonable control
  • Beta or preview features marked as experimental

4. Support response targets

We aim to respond to support requests within the following targets (business days, excluding public holidays):

  • Starter — best effort, typically within 2 business days
  • Professional — target within 1 business day
  • Business — target within 8 business hours
  • Enterprise — per signed agreement (often 4 business hours or less)

Response targets apply to initial acknowledgment via support@balochsahab.com or agreed Enterprise channels.

5. Scheduled maintenance

We may perform maintenance to improve security and reliability. When possible, maintenance is scheduled during low-traffic windows and communicated via email or in-app notice at least 24 hours in advance.

6. Enterprise SLA

Enterprise customers may receive custom uptime commitments, incident communication procedures, and service credits as defined in a separate signed agreement. Contact sales@balochsahab.com for details.

7. Incident communication

During significant incidents affecting core services, we aim to provide status updates through support channels and, where appropriate, email notifications to affected account owners.

8. Limitations

This SLA does not modify disclaimers in our Terms of Service. Service credits, if any, apply only where explicitly stated in an Enterprise contract.
